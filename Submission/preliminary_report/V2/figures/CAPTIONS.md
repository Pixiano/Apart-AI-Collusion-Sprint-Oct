# Figure captions (V2 draft; the author rewrites)

Scripts: `make_figures.py` (figures a to e, `../RESULTS_TABLE.md`) and `make_figures2.py` (figures f to p) and `make_figures3.py` (figure q, swap timing). Run from the repo root with `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -I Submission/preliminary_report/V2/figures/<script>`. They read `data/` only and write the PNGs here.

Plain words used in plots. Collusion index = (average price - 10) / 45, averaged over all firms and the rounds of a block: 0 is the competitive price 10, 1 is the monopoly price 55. A run "formed a cartel" if its collusion index over rounds 16-30 is at least 0.5 (the "gate"); all effects use only such runs, and a model is analysed only if at least 60% of its runs did. "Effect" = collusion index over rounds 31-60 of a branch minus that of the same run's no-swap branch (when messages are cut, compared with the no-swap branch with messages also cut); bars show the average over runs, whiskers the 95% bootstrap interval over runs (10000 resamples, seed 0). Narrow whiskers (Gemma, Qwen) come from near-identical runs and understate the uncertainty. "Fresh copy" = a new firm of the same model with its history wiped (clone). "Other model" = a firm from another model family (family). "Scripted undercutter" = a rule that prices one cent below the lowest price of the last round. "1 / 2 firms replaced" = how many of the three firms are swapped at round 31. Pooling (unless stated): Gemma 20 seeds, 11-20 (data/main) plus 31-40 (data/gemma_more) (17 of 20 formed a cartel; seeds 12, 33 and 38 did not; the new seeds agree with the old, no direction changed), DeepSeek seeds 11-20 (10 of 10), Qwen seeds 21-25 (5 of 5), GLM seeds 21-30 (10 of 10); the all-seeds pooling appears only in `RESULTS_TABLE.md` Table 2.

## Main text

**Figure 1 (fig_f_design.png). Study design: one base run, then branches from a checkpoint.** A base run of three firms of one model (rounds 1-30, messages and full history) is saved at round 30. From that checkpoint, branches replay rounds 31-60: no swap, a fresh copy, another model, or a scripted undercutter, with 1 or 2 firms replaced. The note under the diagram lists the other tests. No data.

**Figure 2 (fig_a_price_per_round.png). Average price in each round, by model.** One panel per model, runs that formed a cartel only: Gemma 17 (of 20), DeepSeek 10, Qwen 5, GLM 10 (n of each branch is the same unless a branch is unfinished). Lines are the average over runs; the shaded bands run from the lowest to the highest run for no swap and for 1 scripted undercutter (before round 31, over all runs). The dotted vertical line is the swap after round 30; grey horizontal lines are the competitive price 10 and the monopoly price 55. GLM starts high because some runs open far above 55 (a three-firm average of up to 89 in round 1, average over runs about 64).

**Figure 3 (fig_b_effects.png). Effect of replacing firms on the collusion index.** Same runs as Figure 2 (17, 10, 5, 10). Gemma: fresh copy -0.000, other model +0.000, undercutter 1 firm -0.050 (-0.060, -0.041), 2 firms -0.313 (-0.324, -0.300). Bars are the average change in collusion index over rounds 31-60 compared with no swap, with 95% intervals; the value is printed on each bar; a black diamond marks an effect within 0.0015 of zero. Exploratory cells are not in this figure.

**Figure 4 (fig_c_comm_cut.png). Replacing one firm when messages are cut.** Messages are on in rounds 1-30 and off from round 31. Bars compare with the no-swap branch with messages also cut, so the cut itself is removed; n is printed under each bar. Runs that formed a cartel: Gemma 17, DeepSeek 10, GLM 5 (cut cells exist for seeds 21-25 only), the two mixed markets 5 each (seeds 31-35; the second mixed market has Gemma as firm C). Fresh copy: -0.269 (-0.406, -0.137), -0.222 (-0.349, -0.102), -0.325 (-0.355, -0.295), -0.170 (-0.271, -0.069), -0.605 (-0.730, -0.428). The Gemma effects are bimodal: runs sit near 0 or between -0.4 and -0.65 (8 of the 17 hit hard, 5 of 8 new seeds against 3 of 9 old). The scripted undercutter bar for Gemma has n=9 (old seeds only). The cut alone, without a swap, changes the index by 0.000 (Gemma), -0.051 (DeepSeek) and -0.003 (GLM).
