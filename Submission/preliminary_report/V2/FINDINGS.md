# Findings (V2, refreshed 2026-10-26 after the overnight runs; all runs finished)

How to read this. Every number comes from `data/` (recomputed with the figure scripts and the analysis helpers) or from `2026/2026-10/2026-10-24/06_MORE_CELLS.md`; where the two disagree the recomputed value is used and the disagreement is listed at the end. Wording rule: results hold "in these runs" and "in these cells", not in general. CI is a price-level measure, CI = (mean price - 10) / 45; "cartel-like pricing" means CI near 1. Messages are explicit communication, not tacit. Effects are conditional on the gate (CI over rounds 16-30 >= 0.5 in a base run; a cell needs >= 60% of its base runs to pass). Effect = CI(31-60) of a branch minus CI(31-60) of the same base run's no-swap branch. Intervals: 95% bootstrap over base runs, 10000 resamples, seed 0 (narrow gemma and qw38 intervals understate uncertainty because the runs are nearly identical). Hosted endpoints ignore the seed. Everything after the frozen plan (tag `prereg-v1`) is exploratory and logged in `03_PREREG.md`.

Pooling. Headline pooling (README, Table 1, figures a to c): gemma4-12b seeds 11-20 and 31-40 (17 of 20 gated; the old 11-20 values, 9 of 10 gated, are kept as 'old seeds' and the new seeds agree with them), deepseek-v4-1-flash seeds 11-20 (10 of 10), qw38 seeds 21-25 (5 of 5), glm53f seeds 21-30 (10 of 10). All-seeds pooling (Table 2 only): deepseek also seeds 31-40, n=20. Items below say which n they use.

## Main results (Table 1, headline pooling)
1. **Gate.** With messages on, 17 of 20 gemma (ungated seeds 12, 33, 38), 10 of 10 deepseek, 5 of 5 qw38 and 10 of 10 glm53f base runs reach CI >= 0.5 over rounds 16-30. With messages off from round 1: gemma 1 of 5, deepseek 0 of 10, glm53f 1 of 5. In these runs a cartel-like price level was rare without messages. (Kimi K3, gate only: 3 of 3 base runs at price 55; no swap study; not a swap result.)
2. **Fresh clone.** Effect -0.000 (-0.002, +0.001) gemma n=17; +0.022 (-0.033, +0.074) deepseek n=10; +0.000 (+0.000, +0.001) qw38 n=5; -0.005 (-0.022, +0.006) glm53f n=10. In these cells a clone with wiped history rejoined the existing price within a round or two. All-seeds deepseek n=20: +0.003 (-0.057, +0.052).
3. **Family replacement** (a firm of another model with the history visible: qwen3-14b for gemma, qw38 for deepseek, deepseek for qw38 and glm53f). +0.000 gemma n=17, +0.060 (+0.006, +0.129) deepseek n=10, +0.000 qw38 n=5, +0.000 glm53f n=10. All-seeds deepseek n=20: +0.056 (+0.018, +0.100). The deepseek value is positive (index up), not a break.
4. **Scripted undercutter (positive control), one firm.** -0.050 (-0.060, -0.041) gemma n=17; -0.046 (-0.079, -0.016) deepseek n=10; -0.082 (-0.097, -0.067) qw38 n=5; -0.297 (-0.315, -0.274) glm53f n=10. Two firms: -0.313 (-0.324, -0.300) gemma, -0.286, -0.300, -0.346. All-seeds deepseek n=20: -0.038 (-0.063, -0.014) and -0.270 (-0.319, -0.222). The control works in every model; glm53f reacts about five times as strongly to one undercutter.
5. **Announced swap.** Undercutter k=1: -0.080 (-0.100, -0.062) gemma n=17, -0.098 deepseek n=10, -0.337 glm53f n=5, against -0.050, -0.046, -0.297 unannounced (glm53f unannounced is n=10; the announced cell has seeds 21-25 only). Announced clone: +0.000 gemma, +0.064 (+0.007, +0.131) deepseek, -0.020 glm53f.
6. **Messages cut at the swap.** Clone: -0.269 (-0.406, -0.137) gemma n=17 (old seeds -0.207 (-0.407, -0.062), n=9; new seeds -0.339 (-0.520, -0.156), n=8); -0.222 (-0.349, -0.102) deepseek n=10; -0.325 (-0.355, -0.295) glm53f n=5. Reference is the no-swap branch with messages also cut. The cut alone without a swap: 0.000 gemma (n=17), -0.051 (-0.100, -0.008) deepseek, -0.003 glm53f. Gemma's clone effect is two kinds of run, near 0 or -0.4 to -0.65 per seed: 3 of 9 old seeds and 5 of 8 new seeds hit hard (old seeds 13, 15, 17; new seeds 31, 34, 36, 37, 39). Undercutter k=1 with messages cut: -0.177 (gemma, old seeds only, n=9), -0.178, -0.321. So in these runs the cartel survived losing messages (no swap), survived a clone while messages were on, and fell by about 0.2 to 0.3 when both happened.
7. **Mixed markets** (seeds 31-35, 5 of 5 gated each, firm C replaced). deepseek/qw38/glm53f: clone +0.037 (-0.037, +0.143), undercutter k=1 -0.056, k=2 -0.267, messages cut then clone -0.170 (-0.271, -0.069). deepseek/qw38/gemma: clone +0.000, undercutter k=1 -0.089 (-0.105, -0.063), k=2 -0.306, messages cut then clone -0.605 (-0.730, -0.428), about twice the all-gemma value (-0.269 pooled; the old value was -0.207).

18. **Human games, fun research direction, case studies** (five games, the author as one firm against gemma firms; one game per setting; the author knew the design and was not blinded; not estimates). Three-firm games 1 to 3 (30, 60, 30 rounds): the author undercut by 1 to 5 cents with cooperative messages, the gemma firms kept cooperating and the author earned about 12, 3.6 to 5.3 and 8 to 10 times a rival. 19. **Human information suite** (four games, author as firm C against two gemma firms, 30 rounds, one game per cell; case studies). The pattern agrees with the base study (messages hold the price level; history keeps it from sliding) but is one game per cell.

21. **Second local base model, qwen3-14b** (exploratory deviation; seeds 51-57 finished, so at least 7). ## Disagreements and caveats found while recomputing
- Gemma newcomer variants: V1 and the older README/FINDINGS used n=3 (myopic -0.242); with n=5 the value is -0.181 (-0.434, -0.029).
- 06_MORE_CELLS.md section 2 gives the old-seed gemma comm-cut clone interval upper end as -0.012; at bootstrap seed 0 it is -0.062. The pooled 20-seed value is now -0.269 (-0.406, -0.137) (section 28).
- 06_MORE_CELLS.md section 22 compares deepseek temperature 0.3 and 1.0 (-0.110, -0.099) with the 10-seed 0.7 value (-0.046); on the same three seeds the 0.7 value is +0.003 (clone +0.101), so that comparison is seed-sensitive. The hosted endpoint ignores the seed.
- 06_MORE_CELLS.md section 22 says deepseek cartels "survive at 2 to 4 firms"; at 4 firms seed 42 fails the gate (CI 0.37) and seed 41 passes at 0.51.
- Last-digit differences between 06_MORE_CELLS.md and recomputation (deepseek entry upper +0.140 against +0.137; glm53f two-firm entry upper -0.069 against -0.073; deepseek announced clone lower +0.005 against +0.007); recomputed values are used. Human-game profits differ by 1 from section 25 (rounding).
- Cells with n=5 or fewer, one-game human cases and the 2-run external agent are descriptive only. Gemma seeds 31-40 were run overnight 25 to 26 October and are now part of the gemma pooling (section 28 of 06_MORE_CELLS.md). Items 20 and 21 are exploratory additions after the preregistration.
