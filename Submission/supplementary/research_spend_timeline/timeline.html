HOW THE RESEARCH WAS CONDUCTED

AI Collusion Research Sprint, 21 to 26 October 2026 (sprint: 23 to 25 October). Question: does a cartel among language-model pricing agents survive when a firm is replaced? All times IST. Dates and counts come from the repository's daily notes, results note and git log.
Phases:planningfrozen planbuild and pilotmain sweepsexploratory additions and auditfinal runs and report
planning
Wed 21 Oct (before the sprint)
  • Question chosen: "cartel turnover", whether collusion survives when a firm is replaced (sprint question 1.2), with a fallback study. Repository, license and tag pre-sprint created; a local model server set up. No experiment code, runs or results.
frozen plan
Thu 22 Oct (before the sprint)
  • Reading: seven related papers read (abstracts and introductions, then design sections); harness specified on paper; an exploratory ablation list written.
  • Plan frozen: game, gate, swap branches and outcome fixed in the repository before any data (tag prereg-v1).
build and pilot
Fri 23 Oct (sprint day 1)
  • Sprint start (tag sprint-start). Harness and tests written the same afternoon (game, agents, play, swaps, run, analysis).
  • Pilot gate: 5 local models x 3 seeds. Only gemma4-12b formed a cartel (3 of 3); qwen3-14b, gpt-oss-20b, mistral-nemo-12b and phi4-14b priced near the competitive level. Main swap study on gemma started that evening.
main sweeps
Sat 24 Oct (sprint day 2)
  • gemma4-12b swap study finished overnight: seeds 11 to 20, four branches each, 9 of 10 runs formed a cartel; then two firms replaced and announced swaps.
  • Hosted endpoint added (a change from the local-only plan): deepseek-v4-1-flash seeds 11 to 20, then qw38 and glm53f gate and swap studies. Messages off and history off: without messages cartels formed in 1 of 5 and 0 of 10 runs.
  • First exploratory cells: communication cut at the swap, private notes, known end date, mixed-model and eight-firm markets.
exploratory additions and audit
Sun 25 Oct (sprint day 3)
  • Overnight and morning: gap-fill cells, awareness probes (do firms notice a swap?), instructed newcomer prompts, 120-round horizon, 2, 4 and 5 firms, entry of new firms.
  • Hosted credit ran out during the glm53f newcomer cells (21 of 25 branches finished). No hosted runs after that. Round-1 spread test on 50 seeds.
  • Human case studies: 5 games with the author as one firm, then a 4-game information suite (one game per setting, not estimates).
  • Evening audit by four read-only reviews (leaks, numbers, documents, report readiness), fixes applied, and the git history rewritten once to remove material not meant to be public (logged in the disclosure log). Report draft rebuilt as V2.
final runs and report
Sun 25 to Mon 26 Oct
  • Overnight, local GPU only: gemma seeds 31 to 40 added (now 20 seeds, 17 of 20 formed a cartel; no direction changed), swap timing (swap at round 15, 30 or 45), and qwen3-14b as a second local base model, which never formed a cartel (0 of 7 runs) and so cannot test persistence.
  • Report written on the official template; tag submitted on the submitted commit.
