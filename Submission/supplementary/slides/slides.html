Cartels Under Model Replacement

Does a language-model cartel survive a firm swap?

Vatsal Kumar, Independent
AI Collusion Research Sprint, Apart Research
October 2026

The question

Pricing agents built on language models can hold prices above the competitive level.

Models get updated or replaced all the time.

Does the cartel survive when a firm is swapped?

2

The setup

  • 3 firms, repeated pricing game, 60 rounds
  • Checkpoint at round 30
  • Gate: price index (CI) ≥ 0.5

Limitations

  • Only gated runs (CI ≥ 0.5)
  • Toy game, no real market
  • Small samples
  • Scripted defector
  • 60 rounds is short
8

Implications

  • Monitor agent-to-agent messages
  • Test model swaps before deployment
  • Keep auditing across versions
