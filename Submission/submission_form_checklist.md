## Before pressing Submit
- Read the prize terms and Privacy Policy linked on the form. The author is a minor: check age, parental-consent and prize-payout rules, and answer every box truthfully.
- Confirm the PDF is the final version; resubmitting needs the exact same title and details, and new files replace the old ones.
- Check all links open while logged out (repo, video, Zenodo).
- A confirmation email with the project title should arrive; if not, write to the sprint contact address.
